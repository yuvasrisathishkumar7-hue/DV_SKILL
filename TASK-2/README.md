# BUSINESS ANALYSIS

## Discount vs Profit

The scatter plot shows a negative relationship between Discount and Profit. As discount levels increase, more transactions show low or negative profits. This indicates that excessive discounting can reduce profit margins.

## Sales vs Profit

Sales and Profit have a correlation of approximately **0.48**, indicating a moderate positive relationship. Generally, higher sales are associated with higher profit, although higher sales do not always guarantee profitability.

## Category-wise Performance

Technology records the highest total sales among the major categories. It also generates the highest overall profit. Furniture has relatively high sales but comparatively lower profitability, which suggests that its pricing, costs, or discount levels may require further analysis.

## Profit Variation

Profit values show considerable variation across transactions. The presence of both positive and negative outliers indicates that some transactions are highly profitable while others result in substantial losses.

## Numerical Relationships

The correlation heatmap shows that **Sales and Profit** have the strongest meaningful positive relationship. **Discount and Profit** have a negative relationship, while Quantity has only a weak relationship with Sales and Profit.

# 1-PAGE SURVEY REPORT

## RETAIL SALES VISUALIZATION, RELATIONSHIP ANALYSIS & BUSINESS INSIGHTS

### Introduction

The Superstore Sales dataset was analyzed to understand retail sales performance, profitability, discount impact, and relationships among numerical variables. Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn were used for data analysis and visualization.

### Analysis Performed

Bar plots were created to compare Sales and Profit across product categories. A histogram was used to understand the Sales distribution. Box plots were used to analyze Profit variation and identify outliers. A scatter plot was used to study the relationship between Discount and Profit. A correlation heatmap was generated to analyze relationships among Sales, Profit, Quantity, Discount, and Delivery Days.

### Key Findings

The analysis shows that **Technology has the highest total sales and profit** among the major categories. Sales values are highly concentrated at lower values, while a small number of transactions have very high sales values.

Profit values also show considerable variation, with both positive and negative outliers. The Discount vs Profit analysis indicates that higher discounts are generally associated with lower profitability.

The correlation analysis shows a **0.48 positive correlation between Sales and Profit**, indicating a moderate positive relationship. The correlation between **Discount and Profit is -0.22**, indicating a negative relationship.

### Business Insights

Businesses should carefully manage discount levels to avoid unnecessary reductions in profit margins. High-performing categories such as Technology should be monitored and supported. Loss-making transactions should be reviewed to identify possible pricing, cost, and discount-related issues.

### Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates how data visualization and correlation analysis can be used to understand retail performance. The findings can support better decisions related to **pricing, discount management, product categories, and profitability**.
